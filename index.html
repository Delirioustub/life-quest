What it is

Life Quest turns the things you already want to do (training, family time, learning, side projects, leading at work) into quests. Doing them raises your stats, opens chests and grows a living kingdom. Every few weeks, bosses attack. If you've been consistent, you win.

How it works

⚔️Daily questsPick your own quests across 5 stats: Strength, Career, Craft, Home and Wisdom.
📷ProofBigger quests need a photo or a note. Honest logs, real progress.
🎁Loot chestsLogged quests can drop chests: bonus XP, streak freezes, cosmetics, even real-life rewards.
🧠Brain roundsOptional riddles, memory and word games that upgrade your chest.
🏰Your kingdomReal milestones raise buildings. Your camp grows into a village, a town, a city, a castle.
🐉Boss Raid WeekEvery season ends with bosses attacking your kingdom. Consistency is your armor.
🤝Co-op raidsEvery quarter the whole party fights one giant boss together. Everyone sees their share.
💎RelicsWin raids to earn relics and legendary villagers with small perks. Lose and a boss may steal one.

6

Quarterly co-op raids pool the whole family against one huge boss.

The rules

90%+
Guaranteed win. Consistency = quests completed ÷ quests scheduled during the build weeks, for the stat each boss attacks.
70–89%
Contested. Your win chance grows with your score, with a small boost from that stat's level and your relics. The roll is sealed per player and raid, so it can't be re-rolled.
<70%
Loss. The boss steals one relic or legendary villager. With nothing to steal, a building gets cosmetic damage that's repaired next season. You never lose XP, levels or buildings.
🛡️❄️
Shields & freezes count as done. Each daily habit gets 1 free miss per week, and a held streak freeze covers another.
⚔️
Revenge. A stolen item stays with that boss. Beat the same boss in a later raid to take it back. Prologue bonus: everything still stolen comes home on Jan 1, 2027.
⛺
Warning signs. If your last 2 weeks slip below 90%, enemy camps appear around your kingdom. They shrink as you recover.

FAQ

Who can see my data?

Only the game's owner can open the spreadsheet that stores the game. Players never get access to the sheet. Everything goes through the app, which checks every entry.

Where do my proof photos go?

Into a private folder in the owner's Google Drive. They're used to keep logs honest and aren't shared anywhere.

What do I need?

A Google account (any Gmail works) and a phone browser. Open the link, sign in with Google, and add it to your home screen.

Do I have to go to a gym?

No. Everyone picks their own quests. A daily walk can level Strength just like lifting does.

What if I miss days?

Shields and freezes cover the odd miss. You never lose XP or buildings, and a lost raid only costs a relic or some cosmetic damage.

Can I join after the season starts?

Yes. Your consistency only counts from the day you join. If you join after a season's build weeks, you sit that raid out, with no penalty.
